One short rant about a small everyday annoyance: an office printer that jams on page one of a boarding pass, flashes PAPER JAM in cheerful green like it is proud of itself, eats the next three pages, spits out a blank one, and demands toner — while its human misses the flight, rebooks at twice the fare, and keeps paying for the printer's crimes. Running the program drops that rant into the make.fun output folder as UTF-8 text, ready for the gate's 1-to-400-character check.
